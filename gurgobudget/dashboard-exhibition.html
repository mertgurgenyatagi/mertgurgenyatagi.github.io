GurgoBudget · Exhibuild 02 · dashboard only · 20 specimens

Twenty ways to hold six numbers without getting busy

Palette and typeface are settled — Yosun sage and moss, Inter throughout. Nothing here is arguing about colour. The variable is structure: what earns the fold, what nests, and what gets a tap.

Every screen is drawn at 390 × 844 and clipped there. If it didn't fit, it didn't earn its place. This is the Today screen alone — items, wishlist, history and stats are separate pages, built later.

Figures are sample data but internally consistent, so a specimen can be read for sense as well as feel. Same month, same balances, twenty postures.

What every specimen has to carry

  • Daily allowance ₺1.203 as the headline, with Max / Strict / Minimum in support.
  • A projection source picked from six — the four allowances plus two averages. Specimens split between a toggle and a tap-list; that fork is what this exhibition is here to settle.
  • Three always-visible outcome states, never hidden behind a switch: stays solvent, buffer intact, wishlist covered.
  • A log entry point that goes straight to the earliest unlogged day — 24 Aug, two days back. Never "log today"; today is never logged.
  • Whole lira only. No kuruş anywhere.
