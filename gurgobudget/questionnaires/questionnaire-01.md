# Questionnaire 1 — Getting the shape of the budget right

**Q1. When something like rent goes up a little, do you want to just edit the number going forward, or should the app remember what it used to cost before?**

It'd be nice if it remembered.

**Q2. You said Base Income and Base Spend “subside over months.” Does that mean the app should automatically shrink those amounts on its own each month (like a loan slowly paying down), or do they just carry forward unchanged until you manually change them?**

Subside was the wrong word. They persist as they are.

**Q3. What's the real difference between a Wishlist item and a Flex Spend item? For example, does a Wishlist item need some kind of approval or check-off before it counts as spent money?**

Well, the difference in philosophy is that Flex Spend is mandatory. How it interacts with everything else, that's for us to figure out. Hopefully you.

**Q4. Is the Buffer one fixed cushion amount for the whole month, or something that can change from month to month, or even from day to day?**

Fixed.

**Q5. Of the four daily numbers (Max, Daily, Relaxed, Minimum Allowance), which one should be the main number you see first, if any — or do you want to see all four side by side?**

Daily. Also, the "Relaxed" isn't really an appropriate name now that I think about it. Change it to something else. Your call.

**Q6. If you spend less or more than your daily number on a given day, should that change tomorrow's number, or should every day get the same fixed amount no matter what actually happened?**

Fixed amount for the month.

**Q7. Is this app only for planning ahead (deciding what you're allowed to spend), or do you also want to record your actual purchases so it can compare “planned” against “what really happened”?**

The recording aspect is simply that I will log the money I spent in total every day. Well, not quite spent, but the balance difference in my bank account.

**Q8. Is this just for your own personal use, or could more than one person need their own separate login and their own separate budget?**

Just my personal use. No one else will ever use this, guaranteed.

**Q9. When does the month “reset” for you — always the 1st of the calendar month, or a different day you get to choose, like whenever your paycheck lands?**

1st of the month.

**Q10. This site lives inside your GitHub Pages portfolio. Should GurgoBudget just save everything in your browser (no server, no login, nothing else to maintain), or does it need real accounts and a database so your numbers follow you across devices?**

It should track across devices.
