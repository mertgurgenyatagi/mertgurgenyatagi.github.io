# Questionnaire 5 — Screens, records, and edges

**Q1. Picture the app's main sections — something like “Today” (your allowance numbers plus logging today's number), “Setup” (managing your Income/Spend/Wishlist items), and “History” (past months). Does that split make sense, would you cut or merge any of them, or is this fully up to me?**

It's fully up to you.

**Q2. Should a month close automatically the instant the calendar flips over, or do you want some kind of manual “close out this month” action before it's final?**

auto

**Q3. Once a month is closed and in the past, should it stay frozen exactly as it was calculated back then, or should a later change to a Base Income/Spend item ever be allowed to reach back and alter how a closed month looks?**

yeah sure, the user has full agency

**Q4. Can you correct a logged day's number only within the current, still-open month, or should past, already-closed months be editable too?**

the user has full agency

**Q5. For Base Income and Base Spend items, is a name and a single monthly amount all you need, or do you also want to record something like a specific due date each month (e.g. rent due on the 5th)?**

no need

**Q6. Same question for Flex Income, Flex Spend, and Wishlist items — just a name and an amount, or is there another piece of information you'd want attached to each one?**

no need

**Q7. Do you want to see a day-by-day list of everything you've logged so far this month, or is the running ahead/behind total enough without needing to scroll through individual days?**

I want to see it. I want all the info available somewhere no matter how tucked away

**Q8. For the ahead/behind number, is a single line (“340 ahead”) enough, or would a simple day-by-day trend be worth seeing?**

okay, here's the design philosophy: important, main things are easily accessible, but also pages exist for all kinds of shit like graphs, stats etc

**Q9. Do you ever expect to add a Wishlist item before you know its exact price (a rough guess you'd firm up later), or will you always know the exact amount when you add one?**

yeah it goes for all items. we're keeping it flexible claude

**Q10. When you buy something from the Wishlist, is that a distinct action — like marking it “bought” so it changes status — or does it just get deleted from the Wishlist while the amount gets folded into that day's raw balance log, like we discussed earlier?**

it gets crossed out and the amount is deducted from the "money saved up this month"
